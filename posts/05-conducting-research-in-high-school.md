## The Reality of Research vs. Competition Math

Competition math rewards speed and cleanness — a correct answer in 3 minutes beats a correct answer in 30. Research is almost the opposite:

*   Most approaches fail, and failing isn't a sign you're doing it wrong — it's the job.
*   Progress is often "I ruled out an entire approach" rather than "I proved the theorem."
*   You have to get comfortable writing things down formally, even half-finished ideas, because you *will* forget the details of an argument you had three weeks ago.
