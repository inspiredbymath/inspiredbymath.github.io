Why it's Fibonacci

To reach step N, your last move was either a single step (from N–1) or a double step (from N–2). Every way of reaching the top therefore splits cleanly into those two groups, so

ways(N) = ways(N–1) + ways(N–2)

— which is exactly the rule that generates the Fibonacci sequence. Blue segments below are +1 moves; terracotta segments are +2 moves.
